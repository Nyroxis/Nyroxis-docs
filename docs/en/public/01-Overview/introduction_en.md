# Introduction

Nyroxis is a privacy-first, lightweight cybersecurity platform designed to give individuals and professionals the same level of visibility and protection that enterprises receive — without the complexity, cost, or cloud dependency.

In modern digital life, personal and unmanaged endpoints have become the weakest link. The personal laptop of a CEO, a family tablet sharing the same Wi-Fi as a senior administrator, the late-night browsing session of a judge or a lawyer — these devices exist outside corporate security perimeters, unmonitored and exposed. Attackers know this. They exploit it deliberately.

Nyroxis provides a clear, simple answer: **local security intelligence**, directly on the device, with encrypted storage, a multi-layer detection engine, and AI-assisted analysis.

## The Gap Nyroxis Solves

While organizations benefit from SOC teams, SIEM platforms, and 24/7 monitoring, individuals and small professional environments do not.

This creates a critical gap:
- No visibility into what processes, connections, or actions occur on the device
- No alerting when suspicious activity happens
- No forensic evidence when an incident occurs
- No way to detect multi-stage or stealthy attacks

Nyroxis bridges this gap by offering a structured, intelligent, and offline-capable security layer — one that monitors, detects, and preserves forensic evidence without ever sending data outside the device.

## Our Approach

Nyroxis focuses on four core principles:
- **Local-first security** — all logs and analysis remain on the device
- **Multi-layer detection** — 27 detection rules, 12 correlation rules, 2 chain rules, and local AI
- **Forensic readiness** — tamper-resistant, forensic-grade encrypted evidence
- **Human-friendly visibility** — clear dashboards and explanations for any user

The result is a system that brings enterprise-grade awareness and detection into a form suitable for families, professionals, executives, and cybersecurity practitioners.
