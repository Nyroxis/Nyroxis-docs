# Target Personas

Nyroxis is designed for real people who need stronger protection than traditional consumer security tools can provide.
Below are the primary personas the platform is built for.

---

## Executives & Senior Management

Executives and public figures are frequent targets of:
- Credential theft and spear-phishing
- Social engineering and targeted malware
- Surveillance and network interception
- Long-term, low-noise intrusions

Their personal laptops — used at home, in hotels, and on travel — are rarely covered by corporate EDR.
Nyroxis gives them local, silent visibility into suspicious activity without relying on external cloud services or corporate monitoring.

---

## Legal Professionals, Judges & Public Servants

Professionals in sensitive roles need:
- Strong privacy — no cloud transmission of case-related activity
- Tamper-resistant event logging for incident investigation
- Early detection of surveillance or compromise attempts

Nyroxis helps protect devices that store legal documents, case files, contracts, and confidential communications — with forensic-grade evidence always ready.

---

## Doctors, Journalists & High-Privacy Professions

Professionals handling regulated or sensitive information — medical records, source protection, financial data — cannot risk cloud-based monitoring tools.

Nyroxis provides:
- Fully local detection and storage
- No behavioral data leaving the device
- Protection without third-party access

---

## Families & Everyday Users

Families often lack tools that explain:
- What is happening on their computer
- Whether something suspicious is occurring
- What risks exist for children and teenagers

Nyroxis provides simple visibility, clear alerts, and peace of mind — without requiring technical knowledge.

---

## Cybersecurity Professionals & SOC Analysts at Home

Experts want:
- A personal SIEM-style event timeline
- Behavioral and correlation insights
- Local encrypted logging with forensic integrity
- Extensible rule engine for custom detections
- No cloud dependencies

Nyroxis gives them a structured, professional environment for monitoring their own devices — with the ability to write and deploy their own detection rules.

---

## Remote Workers & Frequent Travelers

Travelers and remote professionals face:
- Untrusted networks and compromised routers
- Evil-twin Wi-Fi and local interception attempts
- Increased credential theft exposure

Nyroxis offers a portable, autonomous, offline-first security layer that travels with the user and requires no internet to function.

---

## Independent Contractors & Consultants

Freelancers working with sensitive client data need:
- Protection without corporate surveillance
- No cloud telemetry or remote access
- Detection that works on personal devices

Nyroxis provides local-only, client-confidential protection — ideal for multi-client professional environments.

---

## Summary

Nyroxis is built for anyone who needs clarity and protection at the personal level — from families to high-profile professionals, from remote workers to cybersecurity practitioners, and from independent consultants to senior executives whose personal devices are never truly off the clock.
