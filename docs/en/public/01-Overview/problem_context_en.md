# Problem & Context

Cybersecurity has evolved, but personal protection has not.
While enterprises use SOC teams, SIEM platforms, and advanced monitoring, individuals and small professional environments remain exposed.

## Personal Endpoints Are Now Prime Targets

Attackers increasingly target:
- Executives and high-profile individuals
- Lawyers, judges, doctors, and professionals with sensitive data
- SOC administrators and IT leaders whose personal credentials can unlock enterprise infrastructure
- Families and children
- Remote workers and travelers

These devices often contain:
- Private communications and strategic decisions
- Financial and legal information
- Access to corporate accounts and systems
- Personal photos and documents

Yet they lack any structured monitoring or detection capability.

## The Structural Blind Spot

Home networks are not monitored. Personal laptops are not enrolled in corporate EDR systems. Family devices share Wi-Fi with sensitive professional communications.

A single compromised personal device can become the silent entry point into critical infrastructure, executive communications, or sensitive legal proceedings. The attacker does not need to breach the firewall — they only need to reach the person behind it.

## Lack of Visibility = Increased Risk

Most users cannot see:
- What processes are running on their device
- What connects to the internet
- What files are modified
- Whether malware or persistence mechanisms exist
- Whether someone is spying on them

Without visibility, attacks remain unnoticed until it is too late.

## Why Traditional Tools Are Not Enough

Antivirus tools focus on signature-based detection.
But modern threats rely on:
- Living-off-the-land techniques
- Slow and stealthy behavioral patterns
- Fileless attacks
- Credential theft
- Multi-stage intrusion sequences

These techniques require **behavioral visibility and multi-layer detection** — not just scanning.

## The Core Problem

There is no personal-level visibility layer equivalent to enterprise SIEM.
No lightweight, privacy-respecting, offline-capable solution designed specifically to protect the personal endpoints of high-value individuals.

Enterprise tools are too heavy, too intrusive, and too dependent on corporate infrastructure.
Consumer antivirus is too shallow.
