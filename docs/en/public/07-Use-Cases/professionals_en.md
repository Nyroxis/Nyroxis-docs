# Professionals in High-Privacy Fields

Nyroxis is ideal for professionals who work with confidential, sensitive, or regulated information and need strong security without exposing client or case data to the cloud.

---

## Who This Category Includes

- Lawyers and legal professionals
- Judges and magistrates
- Journalists and investigative reporters
- Psychologists and therapists
- NGOs and human-rights activists
- Researchers and academics
- Medical professionals
- Financial consultants and advisors
- Private investigators

These professionals cannot risk cloud storage, third-party monitoring, or behavioral profiling of their work devices.

---

## 1. Local-Only Security for Sensitive Work

Nyroxis ensures:
- No external servers involved in any function
- No cloud processing or storage
- No telemetry or usage tracking
- No remote monitoring by any third party

All activity stays local, encrypted, and private.

This is essential for maintaining legal privilege, source protection, medical confidentiality, and professional ethics.

---

## 2. Protects Against Targeted Threats

Professionals in high-value roles are increasingly targeted. Nyroxis helps identify:
- Hidden monitoring tools and spyware
- Credential theft attempts
- Malicious scripts and fileless attacks
- Suspicious network activity and beaconing
- Slow, targeted intrusions designed to evade traditional tools

Detection and correlation findings are available without exposing any client or case information.

---

## 3. Multi-Layer Detection

Nyroxis Intelligence evaluates events across:
- 27 detection rules — known threat patterns
- 12 correlation rules — multi-event patterns over time
- 2 chain rules — multi-stage attack sequences

Combined with local AI/ML anomaly detection, this provides coverage against both known and behavioral threats.

---

## 4. No Client Data Collection

Nyroxis **never** collects:
- Case files or legal documents
- Client communications or messages
- Medical records or patient data
- Source identities or journalist notes
- Photos, audio, or video
- Any content unrelated to security events

Only technical security events are used for protection.

---

## 5. Reliable Protection During Travel

Professionals often work in hotels, on public Wi-Fi, in remote areas, and on personal devices without corporate network protection.

Nyroxis ensures continuous protection even without an internet connection — fully air-gap capable.

---

## Summary

Nyroxis provides strong, private, offline protection for professionals who handle the most sensitive information — without cloud exposure, without data risk, and without compromise to professional confidentiality.
